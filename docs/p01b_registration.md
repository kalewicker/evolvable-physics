# P01b: full-pilot registration and frozen detector reporting

Registered 2026-10-19, before new detector reporting. P01b adds a reporting executable and validates it on the already disclosed 64 P01a trajectories (IDs 2000..2015, four initial states, 64 epochs), without regenerating or extending them. Six seed-0 integration fixtures and the seed-0 `attached_inert` detector fixture check positive, negative and inconclusive reporting. Existing P01a forced cap controls check censored-prefix behavior; separate tiny constructed controls force binding, match and world-size caps, without new random draws. No new random worlds or full P01 slots run in this milestone.

## Full P01 development budget

`p01_protocol.json` freezes the future pilot's 128 law draws (IDs 3000..3127) × four initial states × 1,000 epochs, in proposal-major order. They are disjoint from P01a and remain development data. Generator v1, environment profile, native caps, detector thresholds and lack of repair stay unchanged. Duplicate laws and inapplicable rules are charged and retained. These draws do not provide independent evidence about all possible physics.

Budgets are 8 GiB of epoch traces, 12 GiB of total campaign artifacts, 1 hour of aggregate process CPU and 2 hours of elapsed time. The 12 GiB total includes source copies, summaries, analysis reports, checksums and logs; retain 16 MiB for metadata. A full 32 MiB next-worker trace plus bounded 16 MiB Rust report, 1 MiB Python audit, request/summary/log allowances must fit before launching a slot. Reserve the complete next slot's 100-second worker/analysis wall allowance against both remaining wall and process-CPU budgets. These conservative launch reservations do not guarantee bounds on OS or watchdog overhead; stop launching as soon as any remaining budget cannot admit a complete slot. Separately check budgets between stages. The future runner must enforce bounded output for every child, not just the native worker.

Charge total process CPU to parent self time plus reaped child usage, including generation, worker, Rust replay/detection, Python independent replay/detection and RSS probes; never add per-worker CPU again to that total. Builds and readiness checks occur before campaign time starts and are separately logged. Sealing is charged to total elapsed time and storage, with metadata reserved in advance; report any unavoidable overshoot, and do not claim an exact CPU or wall bound. Artifact accounting counts file bytes, not filesystem allocated blocks. No silent deletion of unfavorable outputs is permitted to regain a budget.

Per native worker: 10-second wall watchdog, sampled RSS 128 MiB, 200,000 total events, 32 MiB epoch trace. Rust reporting: 30 seconds, sampled RSS 512 MiB, output <=16 MiB. Independent Python audit: 60 seconds, sampled RSS 1 GiB, output <=1 MiB. Both analysis stages have their own failures/outcomes. All run limits include the whole intended input; a timeout never becomes a negative detector label.

The selected memory policy is **sampled RSS**, accepted for this development pilot only. The target polling interval is 10 ms plus observation overhead. Short peaks may be missed, overshoot is possible, and child final peak RSS is reported separately. This is not a hard memory guarantee, nor evidence of bounded memory in arbitrary laws. Stronger enforcement, larger budgets, changed distribution or new thresholds require a separately registered version before use. This policy is a routine engineering choice for the authorized development scope; it does not authorize confirmatory E01 work.

The P01a mean linear trace scenario was 5.91 GB; the observed-maximum-rate scenario was 14.91 GB. The 8 GiB trace budget intentionally may stop the panel or censor slow/large runs. Such an outcome is informative. No expansion, substitute seeds, continuation of capped runs or rerolls are allowed. The slow tail is the observed per-slot upper cost distribution and all cap frequencies, not a fitted extrapolation with failed runs removed. Report mean, median, nearest-rank p95 and maximum cost for started runs, as well as cap/unfinished counts over all 512 slots. Report lower observed cost for interrupted runs and mark censoring; never treat it as an uncensored runtime. Do not call the observed maximum a bound.

## Detector/reporting contract

`pilot-report REQUEST RUN_DIRECTORY` reads a worker's complete newline-terminated canonical epoch records and its separate summary. It checks request schema, epoch/event/byte counts, canonical record bytes, final identity, and the next rejected epoch for a cap. It invokes the unchanged full-environment detector adapter, which replays reference matching and credits native physical changes only. All connected/coactivity samples, tracks, excluded-large counts, ambiguity, disagreement and turnover are retained. `maintenance_status` is always `not_assessed`; turnover is not maintenance.

A `complete` worker must supply exactly the requested horizon before a full-horizon operational AP label is exported. A verified event/trace/matcher/world-cap prefix exports `full_horizon_ap: null`, `coverage: censored_prefix`, and a separate descriptive `prefix_detection`. Even a prefix AP support is not a full-horizon result. An interrupted/partial trace, missing summary, dishonest cap reason, count mismatch or replay failure is an infrastructure error, with no detector endpoint. The future campaign runner must retain that outcome and all unstarted slots. No early AP positive allows stopping or extension.

Full-horizon AP labels have denominator all registered slots, with unmeasured/censored/inconclusive cells explicit. Also show a complete-case descriptive table without turning it into prevalence. Report all four initial states per law, and count a law as having an operational AP-positive state only when a full independently audited state supports it; do not pool the four states as independent law draws. There is no primary hypothesis test or discovery endpoint in this development pilot. No selection or archive search is performed; no E01 censoring/statistical rule is admitted here.

P01b independently compares Rust reports against the frozen Python environment detector on every retained P01a run and seven fixed controls. Forced event/trace caps, equality at boundaries, missing summaries, partial lines, forged `complete`, forged cap reasons and altered detector output must be rejected or censored as specified. No requirement is imposed on the generated AP labels themselves: disagreement must remain inconclusive. The gate concerns reporting fidelity, not favorable physics.

## Readiness boundary

A successful P01b receipt admits this registration and reporting contract. Full P01 execution readiness still requires a sealed campaign runner that enforces these multi-stage budgets, output ceilings, fixed denominator, per-stage failure accounting and immutable evidence publication. P01a's runner is frozen and only admits its own panel; it must not be repurposed by editing its protocol. The next bounded implementation is that new runner and its forced-budget tests. No full pilot runs in P01b. P01b source is frozen separately; all P00/P01a contracts remain byte-identical.
