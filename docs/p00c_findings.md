# P00c environment implementation and verification findings

Date: 2026-10-17. Engineering component: explicit supply, extraction, and local transport passed. Full P00: incomplete. Scientific acceptance: untested.

Publication note: compact receipts and test logs are included under [evidence](evidence/README.md). Raw run histories, checkpoints, and original source snapshots remain local and are regenerated by the validation runner.

## Implementation

Added the separately versioned `p00c-local-driven-v1` environment wrapper around the unchanged native closed-world kernel. It executes scheduled channels by channel ID before native rewriting and atomically commits the whole epoch. Explicit injection/extraction ledgers distinguish imported activation from native conservation and dissipation. Resource transport requires a directed binary Contact edge.

Successful environment actions receive event IDs and writer provenance; insufficient-resource attempts have separate records without spending resources or allocating events. Whole-wrapper checkpoints preserve schedule, phase boundary, ledgers, native rules/state, and event history references. The old native-only loader rejects environment checkpoints.

The checkpoint publication helper was factored out for reuse without changing its no-overwrite behavior. No new dependency was added. Environment semantics and fixed fixture definitions are in [environment.md](environment.md).

## Verification strength

The independent Python implementation recomputes channel eligibility/order, resource actions, skipped attempts, ledger updates, event allocation, dependency parents, and full wrapper hashes before independently replaying the native substep. It also checks the fixture law/configuration definitions and expected engineering outcomes.

Rust tests verify rollback of the complete epoch after native match-budget failure, environment arithmetic overflow, or event-ID overflow. Additional checks cover schedule/route rejection, reversed direction, ledger mismatch, storage-order independence, explicit dependency propagation from supply through transport/extraction into native firing, and unchanged native evolution in the zero-channel wrapper.

Eight tampered trace/configuration cases were rejected: ledger, action order, route, parent IDs, prepared native state, schedule, missing native event, and falsely reported skipped action. All four modes resumed in fresh processes from epoch 3 to epoch 8 and matched uninterrupted complete checkpoint payloads. Truncated, altered, and unknown-profile environment checkpoints were rejected, as was passing one to the native-only resume command.

## Limits and next boundary

Environment ordering is deliberately sequential and discrete, not diffusion or a kinetic model. Contact routes and referenced nodes are fixed infrastructure; missing configuration targets reject an epoch. Channels must fit fixed limits; quantities use checked bounded integers. Native activation_total represents net admitted inventory within the wrapper; initial inventory remains separately recorded and immutable in its configuration.

These constructed controls establish correct environmental bookkeeping and local transport in the tested profile. They do not demonstrate persistence, self-maintenance, reproduction, adaptation, or discovery of useful artificial physics.

P00b interventions currently operate on native checkpoints only. Environment-aware branching must bind and preserve the entire wrapper, not extract its native payload and silently lose the schedule. That integration and stronger interrupted-publication recovery tests are the next bounded P00 work. Structure/maintenance detector calibration remains pending, and no P01/E01 run is admitted by this result.
