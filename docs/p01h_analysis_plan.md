2026-10-20. This is a post-hoc engineering diagnosis, planned after P01g's known
outcomes: 64 complete horizons, 142 births, zero additional newborn funding,
descendant construction or recycling, and two inconclusive AP labels. It is not
a preregistered causal experiment. Preserve P01g's failed mechanism gate and
every denominator, label, input, threshold, receipt and source freeze.

## Questions and counting units

1. Census each template and each transfer route per law (eight slots, duplicate
   bodies retained), and committed event forms per trajectory. Report whether
   birth, Structural transfer and recycling coexist in the same law.
2. Compare every birth body with Structural transfer/recycling bodies in that
   law. Incoming newborn edges originate only in birth; node kinds never change.
   Record necessary donor/child kind compatibility. A child's birth state can
   be proved invariant only when no unary SetState/AddState body of its kind can
   first change that state; matching no-op SetState does not change it. Apply
   the same conditional test to a specified donor state at birth. Otherwise mark
   state reachability unresolved, never infer impossibility from finite absence.
3. For every saved pre-epoch Structural edge whose target is an actual earlier
   newborn, cross all Structural-transfer and recycle rules with that ordered
   edge. For child-funded construction, cross all live actual newborns with
   every birth rule. These are rule-binding exposures, not independent samples.
   Distinguish no relevant rule/no relevant edge from a tested binding failure.
4. Classify failed conditions for each exposure: donor/target kind, donor/target
   state, target extra incidence, free matter/activation, integer bounds. Publish
   both overlapping failure counts and an exclusive first-blocker partition in
   that declared order. For birth the resource requirement is matter >=3 and
   activation >=2; for recycling it is edge/node refund availability and Burn
   affordability, with all intermediate additions checked. No operation is run.
5. If all conditions pass, require the exact `(rule,binding)` in the recorded
   candidate list. Require all relevant recorded candidates to be covered by
   exposures. Reconstruct the deterministic footprint-only selection and compare
   its accepted candidates with the recorded events. Partition applicable
   exposures into committed and rejected for a recorded earlier-winner conflict;
   retain witness IDs/footprints, without claiming an intervention benefit.
6. Scan newborn resource/kind/attachment histories and reconcile creation IDs,
   allocator continuation and ledgers. Record whether created members ever had
   construction resources. Report detector eligibility at birth and later from
   original full records; for connected exclusions inspect Structural component
   sizes under the frozen limits. Preserve the two inconclusive cases and
   recompute their saved track-gate reasons without changing labels.

## Evidence and stopping boundary

Produce a new analysis directory with input-audit receipt, complete per-law and
per-cell diagnoses, total counts, source/plan hashes, manifest and checksum
inventory. Validate classifier partitions and malformed-record rejection on
invented records, with transition/sampler calls forbidden. Do not time a new
simulation or treat diagnostic timing as a campaign benchmark.

Separate source-level conditional impossibility arguments from facts about the
retained trajectories, and both from unknown sampling prevalence or life
capability. The absence of a condition along a saved path is not proof that a
different history could not reach it. The diagnostic may identify one concrete
next design boundary, but admits no new distribution, witness run, environment,
sampling, threshold change, or E01. Any later redesign requires its own stated
question and registration.
