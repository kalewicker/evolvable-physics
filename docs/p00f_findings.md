# P00f admission audit and frozen detector transfer findings

Date: 2026-10-19. Panel execution and independent verification: complete. Panel accepted within its declared scope. P00 admission: **not admitted**. Scientific status: untested.

Publication note: compact receipts and test logs are included under [evidence](evidence/README.md). Raw run histories, checkpoints, and original source snapshots remain local and are regenerated by the validation runner.

## Outcome

The unchanged P00e detector matched all predictions on a registered 176-run transfer panel across 11 structure families. Canonical fingerprints and AP labels were invariant across the registered ID encodings where evaluated. The audit also identified two P00 blockers that passing this panel does not resolve: logging-on/off equivalence and full environment-to-detector integration.

Primary evidence: [manifest](evidence/p00f-002/manifest.json), [transfer audit](evidence/p00f-002/generalization_audit.json), [P00 admission audit](evidence/p00f-002/p00_admission_audit.json), `artifacts/p00f-002/generalization/suite.json` (local raw artifact), [Rust test output](evidence/p00f-002/tests.txt), and [checksums](evidence/p00f-002/checksums.json). Registration, source freeze, source/binary hashes, fresh-process audit output, and prior-suite results are retained with the bundle.

[The registration](p00f_registration.md), [machine-readable panel](generalization_panel.json), [source freeze](p00f_freeze.json), and [admission requirements](p00_admission_requirements.json) were written before evaluation. No detector code, threshold, seed, expected label, or fixture generator changed after panel results were observed. The P00e canonical detector-protocol digest remains `c2a471a6573c9e2f0039bb2d305f775d303724166bbc66cc17c961188d4fff80`.

## Fixed denominators and results

11 families × 8 new seeds × 2 ID encodings = 176 runs / 2,112 epochs. There are 88 matched label-invariance pairs; 80 also have bounded exact fingerprints, while the eight seven-node pairs explicitly omit fingerprinting beyond its six-node limit.

| Panel group | Runs | Observed AP outcome |
|---|---:|---|
| Chain-4, ring-6, star-5, arity-3/4 hyperedges | 80 | supported |
| Frozen-6, resource-only cycle, independent templated births | 48 | not_supported |
| One-time fragmentation into active pairs | 16 | not_supported; identity ambiguity retained |
| Seven-node active component | 16 | not_supported; explicitly out of size scope |
| Asynchronous active ring | 16 | inconclusive; estimator disagreement retained |

Zero registered label mismatches and zero ID-invariance failures were observed. The output retains 208 oversized-component sample exclusions (13 observations × 16 runs) and 64 ambiguous identity links. These are not discarded attempts or extra independent runs.

The asynchronous ring has physical activity but no joint-event coactivity linking its nodes, so the current estimator combination remains inconclusive. The seven-node result is a known coverage exclusion, not evidence against active organization. Both are expected limitations retained in the acceptance record.

No new maintenance, reproduction, heredity, or evolution result is claimed. The independent template fixture only shows that two inert constructions do not meet this AP predicate; it does not validate a causal reproduction detector.

## Verification strength and limits

Python independently reconstructs every initial Experiment from the panel registry, replays native transitions, and recomputes complete detector outputs. Rust reference/indexed matching agrees for every epoch. A fresh Python process repeats the panel audit. Seven tampered-evidence cases are rejected: generator, scope, detector summary, fingerprint, expected label, missing event, and unknown encoding.

All 33 Rust test entries pass (32 substantive tests plus the existing recovery helper), along with formatting and Clippy with warnings denied. All earlier fixture suite files remain byte-identical to P00e. The detector source, base protocol, independent analysis code, panel generator, panel protocol, and admission requirements remain at their pre-evaluation frozen hashes.

The panel is a disclosed transfer challenge, not blinded external validation or a random sample of possible physics. Eight seeds and two encodings per family are correlated repetitions, not 176 independent scientific families. Five positive topologies and three negative families are too narrow to estimate population sensitivity/specificity. Counts are reported without a misleading binomial confidence interval over duplicated families.

## Admission audit

| Requirement | Status |
|---|---|
| Fixed-profile replay and exact accounting | Supported in tested fixtures |
| Atomic rejection and checkpoint continuation | Supported in specified cap/failure paths |
| Native/environment interventions and publication boundaries | Supported within recorded recovery limits |
| Fixed detector calibration and transfer panel | Passed within explicit size/identity/coactivity limits |
| Logging enabled/disabled semantic equivalence | **Missing: blocks P00** |
| Full environment-trace detector integration | **Missing: blocks P00** |

Current analysis takes native Experiment/Frame traces. It cannot yet analyze full Environment/Epoch traces with verified phase ordering and correct exclusion of external-only activity. Flattening those traces would lose the very schedule and provenance the environment wrapper was built to preserve.

The runtime always emits its frame/event output; no separate output-disabled execution path has been tested for semantic equivalence. This remains an explicit original-plan requirement.

P01 execution also needs a registered rule generator, profiling/cost instrumentation and budget, and event/time/memory run limits beyond the existing node/edge/matching caps. Those are tracked separately from detector acceptance. P01 and E01 have not started.
