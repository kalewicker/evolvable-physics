# P00e structure and maintenance detector calibration findings

Date: 2026-10-17. Fixed-fixture engineering calibration: passed. General detector validity and scientific discovery: untested. Full P00: incomplete.

Publication note: compact receipts and test logs are included under [evidence](evidence/README.md). Raw run histories, checkpoints, and original source snapshots remain local and are regenerated by the validation runner.

## Result

Implemented offline connected-structure and coactivity proposals, bounded temporal tracking with explicit ambiguity, activity/turnover accounting, estimator agreement on the same candidate, and controlled physical-motif maintenance endpoints.

The independent Python implementation agrees with every emitted proposal, track, agreement pair, activity/turnover count, fingerprint, intervention endpoint, and registered label in this fixed panel. All underlying native traces and intervention branches are independently replayed before their measurements are accepted.

## Denominators and validation

- Nine disclosed fixture families × 16 seeds = 144 primary runs / 1,728 epochs.
- Preassigned development seeds 0–3: 36 runs. Validation seeds 4–15: 108 runs.
- Renewal and passive-turnover families: 32 sources × five arms = 160 maintenance continuations / 1,920 epochs, plus 64 warmup epochs.
- Zero registered label mismatches; 16 estimator disagreements retained as inconclusive.
- All 31 Rust test entries passed (30 substantive tests plus the existing child-process helper). Formatting and Clippy with warnings denied passed.
- Independent detector audit also passed in a fresh Python process.
- Eight altered-evidence cases were rejected: label, activity, membership, turnover, occupancy, intervention request, fingerprint, and disagreement flag.
- All P00a–P00d suites reran successfully; their emitted suite files remain byte-identical to P00d.

Thresholds were fixed before the validation runner. The noise-replacement adversarial family was added during development before the sealed run; no threshold, expected negative outcome, or failing seed was adjusted afterward. These fixture designs are disclosed and the validation seeds repeat their structures; this is not blinded validation or evidence of generalization to unseen families. No statistical sensitivity/specificity estimate over arbitrary worlds is claimed.

## Observed classifications

Every row held for all 16 registered seeds:

| Family | Active persistence | Maintenance |
|---|---|---|
| Frozen pair | not_supported | not_supported |
| Active pair | supported | not_supported |
| Inert object with oscillator | not_supported | not_supported |
| Active pair attached to inert node | inconclusive | not_supported |
| Irrelevant changing state bits | supported | not_supported |
| Wholesale replacement | not_supported | not_supported |
| Transient replacement noise | not_supported | not_supported |
| Aging/removal/repair renewal | supported | supported |
| Passive component turnover | supported | not_supported |

The attached-inert cases are the retained disagreements: connectivity includes the inactive third node and rejects AP; interaction-based extraction finds an active pair. The output preserves both proposals rather than selecting whichever gives a preferred result.

The irrelevant-bits result exposes a limit of the operational AP predicate. Changing state on stable topology satisfies active persistence, but supplies no functional or maintenance evidence. It is explicitly rejected at the maintenance gate.

Wholesale/noisy replacement fails the identity-overlap requirement despite repeated topology. This is a detector sensitivity limit, not a conclusion that fully replacing systems cannot maintain organization. The noise case covers transient component replacement with competing seed-dependent outputs, not all random processes.

## Physical maintenance controls

For renewal, a native leaf-aging rule is followed by leaf removal, leaving a hole that an internal repair rule reconstructs. The target edit disables repair by changing its anchor's state. Baseline/sham/unrelated/rescue retain the physical motif at 8/12 samples; target retains it at 0/12. Matter remains exactly conserved. This supports the registered maintenance endpoint on the engineered fixture.

For passive turnover, components replace themselves without a physical need for repair. Stopping replacement leaves the motif present at 12/12 samples in every arm. The zero physical effect is retained, and maintenance is not supported despite activity and repeated material turnover.

These outcomes use exact topology/type fingerprints, not activity as the intervention endpoint. Thus cessation of an oscillator or unnecessary turnover cannot itself count as loss of a maintained physical organization. The same focal candidate must supply activity, turnover, and the physical intervention effect; unrelated components cannot pool their evidence.

## Scope and limitations

Proposals are restricted to 2–6 nodes and eight consecutive epochs. Identity uses retained-node Jaccard overlap; ambiguous splits/merges and gaps do not pass. Activity uses actual state/type/existence or structural-incidence changes; resource motion and event count alone receive no activity credit. The canonical physical fingerprint ignores states, resources, IDs, and history. These choices are documented restrictions rather than universal individuality criteria.

The current panel does not establish performance on previously unseen size/topology families, arbitrary stochastic noise, broad environment templating, or ecological boundary cases. Full replacement may produce false negatives. Longer-horizon identity, broader event-store failure recovery, and reproduction/heritability assays remain outside this calibration.

No novel world was discovered, and no P01 pilot or E01 census has started. Full P00 remains unaccepted. The next bounded task is a P00 admission audit and a separately frozen detector-generalization panel before broad sampling.
