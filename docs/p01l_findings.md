## Resource and topology boundary

A newborn has (1 matter, 1 activation), versus (3,2) needed for its own birth.
Two unit-matter transfers could close that deficit; an otherwise untouched
original creator after birth has (5,6), enough for transfers with activation
amount 1 or 2. These are conditional budgets, not observed resource histories.
Recycling requires a leaf after edge removal; a descendant must first be
removed. Closed activation starts at 64, with one burned per birth, so even a
compatible type/state cycle would not imply unlimited births or open-endedness.

The [source argument](p01l_source_argument.md) combines all-table necessary
conditions with the immutable topology: newborns lack Contact edges, standalone
edge creation requires Contact, and incoming deletion refunds the creator.
Before any newborn has constructed a child, only material Structural funding
can supply its missing matter. The exclusive partition is 8 tables without
birth, 22 without a type-compatible funding/descendant path, one with only
zero-matter funding on that path, and one excluded by unary states. This is
conditional exact-table exclusion, with source/premise checks; it is not a
formal verification of the runtime and makes no claim about all possible v3
outputs or the kernel's capacity for life.
