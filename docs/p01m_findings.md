## Decision and changed assumptions

The [contract](generator_v4_design.json) and [design review](generator_v4_design.md)
retain v3's unrestricted donor-to-child type map f and introduce a shared
per-kind state map s. Birth creates kind f(P) in state s(f(P)); birth,
Structural funding and recycling use exact s predicates for their kinds.
Structural funding always carries one matter, retaining activation amount 1/2.
The proposal keeps the same operation costs, eight slots, initial world recipe,
closed environment, independent forms/donor kinds/routes and detectors.

This authors state correlations and nonzero material funding. It does not
author the needed bodies, filter laws or provide resource histories. It removes
constructive wildcards, so applicability on originals can decrease. Independent
unary writers may move nodes away from their role states. It is a changed
joint prior, not a superset of v3 or a demonstrated improvement in chemistry.
