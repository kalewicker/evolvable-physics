# P01h: incompatible kinds explain the failed recycling gate

2026-10-20. **None of the 16 retained P01g rule tables has a birth-compatible
ordered kind pair for newborn Structural funding or recycling.** All relevant
funding/recycling exposures failed a kind check before scheduling. The frozen
kernel can express these mechanisms, as P01f showed, but the sampled tables
cannot connect their operation bodies in the required way.

The [source argument](p01h_source_argument.md) explains why these restrictions
hold for the same tables beyond the observed horizon, under the frozen initial
distribution. This is not an impossibility result for the whole v2 sampler,
other laws or the kernel. No law was drawn, no transition was applied and no
world evolved during this diagnosis. The failed P01g continuation gate and both
inconclusive labels remain unchanged.

## Disclosed analysis and verification

The [analysis plan](p01h_analysis_plan.md) was written after P01g's known negative
outcome and before inspecting retained tables/candidate traces. This is post-hoc
engineering diagnosis, not a preregistered causal experiment. The plan, diagnostic
and nine synthetic-record tests were committed as `bdaa38f` before the scan.

The diagnostic verified the original sealed inventory and receipts, checked
unchanged frozen/imported source identities, and scanned all 64 initial states,
8,192 pre-epoch worlds and 7,453 event records. It reconciled every newborn and
mechanism count with the original receipts, compared algebraic applicability
with the saved relevant candidates and checked the recorded footprint scheduler.
Runtime guards rejected sampler, interpreter, candidate-execution and subprocess
calls. Source snapshots, manifest, input audit and results are retained in the
new local `artifacts/p01h-001` bundle.

A separate later [independent record audit](evidence/p01h-001/independent_audit.json)
passed inventory/source/input/count checks and recomputed the law-local kind
matrix directly from retained rule bodies. It did not reuse the diagnostic's
template classifier or state-invariance logic for that matrix. Two additional
tests detect a genuinely compatible pair and exclude Contact-only transfer.
All 11 targeted tests passed. The later audit and test logs are external to the
original diagnostic inventory and bound in publication context.

## Table coverage and observed applicability

There are 12 birth bodies across ten tables, 12 Structural Transfer bodies across
eight tables, 11 Contact Transfer bodies, and 17 recycling bodies across 12
tables. Five tables combine birth and Structural Transfer; seven combine birth
and recycling; two contain all three. Mere body presence was insufficient:
all nine same-law birth/Structural-Transfer combinations and all 15
birth/recycling combinations fail donor and/or child kind compatibility.

| Diagnostic exposure | Rule-binding/pre-epoch observations | Exclusive first blocker |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Structural funding to an actual newborn | 16,598 | 11,822 donor kind; 4,776 target kind |
| Recycling an actual newborn | 18,610 | 16,478 donor kind; 2,132 target kind |
| Birth with an actual newborn as donor | 22,797 | 11,967 donor kind; 2,137 donor state; 8,693 matter |

These are repeated rule-binding exposures, not independent samples. Every
descendant-birth exposure also lacked both matter and activation, including
those whose exclusive first blocker was a kind/state mismatch. Overlapping
conditions are retained separately in the original findings. There were **zero
applicable candidates and zero scheduler losses** for these three newborn
mechanisms; scheduler competition is not the observed barrier to them.

All 142 newborn instances retained exactly one matter and at most one
activation; none reached the required three matter/two activation for birth.
Fifty-six changed state, which does not change their kinds or repair typed
resource routes. The scan counted 17,875 live-newborn pre-epoch observations,
all with an incoming birth edge. It also preserved the absence of relevant
rules: 7,307 newborn-edge epochs had no Structural funding body and 9,314 had
no recycling body. Those are not counted as failed rule-binding exposures.

The full committed-event census remains 4,626 SetState, 2,206 AddState, 370 Burn,
99 Transfer, 142 birth bundles, nine CreateEdge and one DeleteEdge; no recycling
bundle committed. The original complete panel, all zeros and 16 law/four nested
initial-state denominators are unchanged.

## Detector findings preserved

All 142 newborns appeared in interaction proposals at birth. Of these, 104 also
appeared in connected proposals and met joint eligibility. The other 38 had
Structural components of 7–16 nodes at birth, above the unchanged connected
maximum of six. None later became jointly eligible in the retained records.
The component bound was not relaxed, and this visibility diagnosis is separate
from the physical funding/recycling failure.

Slots 29 and 31 remain `inconclusive`. Both connected estimators supported the
original-node pair `[3,4]` for 128 epochs, with zero node births/deaths in that
track. Slot 29 had no interaction proposals in any of 129 samples. Slot 31 had
one four-sample interaction track, failing the unchanged eight-epoch duration
minimum. These records do not justify choosing the connected estimate alone.

## Decision and next bounded task

The diagnosis closes the uncertainty about whether longer runs or different
scheduler seeds could rescue these particular tables' missing typed routes:
under the frozen contract they cannot. The all-table source argument and finite
observations are reported separately; no intervention benefit, sampling
prevalence or life capability is claimed. Scientific status remains untested.

generation**, including whether children can themselves match donor patterns.
