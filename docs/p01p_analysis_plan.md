   reasons. Rule IDs may be reused in different phases; P, C and G denote
   distinct nodes even if their kinds coincide. Never pool bodies across laws.
2. Compute unary-state possibilities using signed-i32 interval closure. Only
   SetState/AddState writers of the role kind apply; checked addition cannot
   wrap or clamp. Preserve immediate common-state tests separately from ordered
   phase tests. Filter a phase's accepted state set before taking the next
   closure. Arbitrary finite unary sequences ignore scheduler/competition and
   thus give permissive state conditions, not histories.
3. For the conditional isolated branch, fix the order: original P births C;
   P funds C twice; C births G; C's outgoing edge to G is cleaned up; P recycles
   C. P's predicates follow birth -> funding -> creator recycling. C's follow
   newborn -> funding -> own birth -> cleanup -> creator recycling. G starts
   in its exact birth state and must meet its cleanup predicate. Examine every
   eligible cleanup body (form6 or form7), retaining kind/state failures.
4. Verify the source topology premises. No template creates Contact edges;
   standalone Structural creation requires an existing Contact edge. Newborns
   have no Contact incidence. They obtain an incoming creation edge from birth,
   and no outgoing edge before their own first construction under these
   templates. Creation IDs are fresh; refunds go to the ordered source.
5. Separate the two cleanup routes. Form6 deletes C->G and refunds its edge
   matter to C; G survives as an orphan. Form7 additionally requires G to have
   no remaining incidence, deletes/refunds G and burns one activation. C can
   become a leaf after its outgoing edge is removed **without deleting G**.
   P->C must survive for P's recycling body. Extra incidence, earlier orphaning
   and interference are unresolved outside this isolated-family scenario.
6. Record closed-form conditional resource ledgers, not mutable simulated
   worlds. P starts at (8,8), C/G each at (1,1). Birth requires (3,2). Two unit
   matter transfers with activation a in {1,2} leave P=(3,6-2a) and
   C=(3,1+2a). C's birth leaves C=(0,2a-1), G=(1,1). Cleanup6 leaves
   C=(1,2a-1) and orphan G=(1,1); subsequent P recycling leaves P=(6,4).
   Cleanup7 leaves C=(3,2a-1), deletes G, and subsequent P recycling leaves
   P=(8,4). Total activation dissipated is respectively 3 or 4; the surviving
   orphan holds activation1 in the edge-only route. These four fixed arithmetic

7. Across all 128 initial inventories, retain kind/state seed counts and unary
   access conditions for each birth body. Do not enumerate concrete bindings.
